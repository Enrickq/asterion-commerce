## Before using the captions

Record the functional footage and narration, lock the edit, then align every cue to the actual spoken words. The current gaps and reading durations are planning estimates. Review the exported video with subtitles enabled and correct timing, line breaks and any narration changes before upload.

Keep the public €1,480 simulation distinct from the historical €1,650 Gemini/PayPal Sandbox evidence. Persistent simulation, Sandbox and fixture-weather disclosures belong in the video overlay; the spoken-word SRT does not replace them. Do not present historical stills or inspected records as footage of a new private execution.

The draft captions end before 2:50, use sequential non-overlapping timestamps, and preserve the script transcript. Those checks establish file consistency, not completion of a video or compliance of a future upload.
