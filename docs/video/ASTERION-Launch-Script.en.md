## Edit and delivery direction

- Full-screen application and original motion design. Remove the previous right-hand review panel and the persistent “review cut / synthetic voice” header. Use concise lower-third context labels where they change how a result must be interpreted.
- Let the opening, the refusal and the closing line breathe. Use an original instrumental score and original sound effects. Duck them under narration. Drop the score briefly on the failed-condition result; resume on the recorded proof.
- Keep the app's amounts and states legible. Kinetic typography explains the architecture; it must not manufacture an app result, provider request or transaction.
- The public €1,480 clear and cloudy flows are different agreements. Their payments, negotiation and weather are simulated. The clear run must not appear to be reversed by changing its weather.
- At 1:44, identify a scene change to historical private results. Three actual Gemini requests produced the €1,650 agreement within a €1,700 ceiling; that agreement completed PayPal Sandbox capture and three individually confirmed Payouts. Sandbox uses test funds. Inventory and settlement weather remained fixtures.
- One organizer is the merchant. Supplier Payouts are separate operations after capture, using its available balance. Do not depict an atomic split, escrow or a smart contract installed inside PayPal.
- A receipt's offline check demonstrates internal consistency. The read-only MET Norway forecast supports planning; it cannot authorize capture. Complete local observations and actual operators remain the next field milestone.
- Captured UI steps may be edited for time. This film is not continuous footage of a new private Sandbox execution. No public upload is authorized by this script; owner review remains pending.
- Accessibility: aligned English captions; sufficient contrast; no rapid strobing. Keep sources and attribution in the accompanying description and visible where relevant.
